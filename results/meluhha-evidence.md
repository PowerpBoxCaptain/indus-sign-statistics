## C. What step 3 gives the language question

- The outside record of the language is, on the leading specialists' own reading (item 5, still
  secondhand), two personal names: Nanaza and Samar. Both are short and fit many languages; two names
  cannot choose a family. Proposed etymologies of the names or of "Meluhha" itself (e.g. links to
  Sanskrit mleccha) are claims by others, not read here: NULL.
- Everything else Mesopotamia says about Meluhha is in Sumerian: its goods (timber, carnelian, gold,
  tin-bronze), its animals (bulls, francolins, a bird called ha-ia3), boats, and "black land".
- So the sound-level handle from outside is as thin as it could be. A real step forward would need
  either more Meluhhan names (a CDLI search for me-luh-ha, when it is back, and the Ur III texts
  behind items 6 and 7) or a bilingual seal, which nobody has found.
